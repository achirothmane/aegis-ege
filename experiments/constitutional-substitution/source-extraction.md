## Deliberately unmapped

- A.NAMES individually: the extract supplies names but not individual executable
  definitions. No separate Taqwa/Amana/Sidq/Huda/Maslaha/non-harm primitive.
- Religious validity, scholarly approval and financial supervisory-board
  authority: preserved as source standing; no fixture signature is represented
  as qualified religious review or certification.
- Gharar: no formalisation, Boolean compliance predicate or entropy equivalence.
- Global optimisation of benefit versus harm, all-purpose dignity metrics,
  complete theology and moral priority: no supplied decision procedure.
- The supporting concept note: provenance only, no normative expansion.

## Scope of disagreement

The A/B disagreement concerns these transparently compiled **operative fragments**
plus the common baseline authority. Section 5's silence about ordinary approvals
for a non-safety effect is not a ruling that the full working draft authorizes it.
Likewise the minimal A validation topology is not a claim of globally sufficient
approval under the full Orionech framework.
